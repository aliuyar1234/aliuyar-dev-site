# DATA.md: language-neutral-or-language-specific

`data.mjs` (about 80 KB, pure ESM with no imports and no Node APIs) holds the real results of *Language-Neutral or Language-Specific? Disentangling Multilingual LLM Subspaces with Naturalistic fMRI* (Uyar, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.19185476) for an isometric slice-stack brain figure.

Every value comes from the paper repo `/Users/user/Developer/personal/aliuyar1234/language-neutral-or-language-specific` (abbreviated `REPO` below), read-only. The only changes are the documented resampling and quantization steps. `data.mjs` was generated by `build.py` (with `common.py`) in `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-user/e53d1c96-0028-496b-a660-03fceeab7767/scratchpad/site/brain-data/language-neutral-or-language-specific/`, and `verify.py` / `check.mjs` in the same folder re-derive values from the source files. That folder is a session scratchpad, so it will not persist. The algorithm is described in full below so the file can be rebuilt.

## Read this first: what the brain data is and is not

1. **The maps are ROI-projected, not voxelwise.** Each `roi_projected_maps/<model>/<lang>/layer_XX/*.nii.gz` fills every voxel of an atlas parcel with that ROI's group-mean held-out Fisher-z at one representative layer. The repo's `manifest.json` says: *"ROI means projected onto atlas parcels for descriptive visualization; not voxelwise encoding maps."* The paper says the same (Sec. 4.7): *"rendered as an ROI-projected atlas visualization rather than a voxelwise encoding map … must not be interpreted as a voxelwise inferential whole-brain result."* As a result, values are piecewise-constant per parcel, and the caption or readout must not call them voxelwise.
2. **`brain_mask.nii.gz` is not a whole-brain mask.** It is `atlas > 0` on the ROI-restricted atlas (`src/brain_subspace_paper/viz/figures.py::_write_roi_projected_maps`, `roi/targets.py::_resampled_atlas_and_metadata`). That atlas keeps only the 18 analysis ROIs. So the mask and its outlines trace the **union of the 18 ROI parcels** (temporal poles, STG, MTG, angular gyrus, IFG, precentral gyrus, occipital pole), not a brain silhouette. The repo holds no whole-brain or cortex mask.
3. **Maps exist only at the representative layers**, which are XLM-R layer 7 (depth 0.5833) and NLLB encoder layer 8 (depth 0.6667), for each of EN/FR/ZH. That makes 6 maps. The paper (Sec. 4.7) says: *"The representative layers were XLM-R layer 7 and NLLB layer 8, chosen by semantic-family shared advantage."*
4. **NLLB brain results exist only for encoder layers 4, 6 and 8.** The paper logs this as an implementation deviation (Sec. 3.3 / 3.7). Its layer curves therefore have 3 points, against 13 for XLM-R.

## Coordinate system

- Grid: the LPPC derivative BOLD grid (paper Sec. 3.1: MNIColin27 space), shape 73 × 90 × 74, with 2 mm isotropic voxels. The affine is diagonal +2 mm, and voxel (0,0,0) is centred at (−72, −106, −64) mm (`meta.voxel0Mm`).
- Axes are RAS mm: +x = subject right, +y = anterior, +z = superior. The L ROIs all have x < 0 and the R ROIs all have x > 0, which was checked against the repo's hemisphere rule `x<0 → L`. When drawn with +x to the right and +y up, a plate is viewed from above (neurological convention).
- All outline and cell coordinates are in mm in this frame.

## Exports, field by field

### `meta`
Static strings and numbers: paper title, space, `voxelMm` 2, `gridShape` [73,90,74], `voxel0Mm`, plus `maskNote`, `mapNote` and `units` carrying caveats 1 and 2 above. Source: the NIfTI header/affine of `REPO/outputs/stats/roi_projected_maps/*/*/*/brain_mask.nii.gz`. All 6 maps share the same affine and mask (checked).

### `families`
`["semantic","auditory","control"]`. Source: `REPO/configs/rois.yaml` (`roi_families`).

### `rois[18]`: `{id, name, family, hemi, atlasLabel, nVoxels}`
- `id` 0..17 = repo `roi_index` − 1, in the exact order `roi/targets.py::ROI_SPECS` builds it: pMTG, AG, TemporalPole, IFGtri, Heschl, pSTG, aSTG, Precentral, OccipitalPole, each L then R.
- `name`, `family` match `group_level_roi_results.parquet` columns `roi_name`, `roi_family`. `atlasLabel` is the Harvard-Oxford cortical label from `ROI_SPECS` (atlas `cort-maxprob-thr25-2mm`, nearest-neighbour resampled to LPPC space, per `rois.yaml` and paper Sec. 3.5).
- `nVoxels`: the 3D voxel count of each parcel in the repo's resampled atlas, recovered as described under "Parcel identity" below. Total 27 305 = mask voxel count. Examples: L_Precentral 4296, R_Precentral 4149, L_TemporalPole 2350, R_aSTG 268.

### `grid`
`{cellMm: 6, cellVoxels: 3, xEdge0: -73, yEdge0: -107, scale: {shared: 0.0015, specific: 0.0015, delta: 0.001}}`. Cell `(i, j)` covers x ∈ [−73 + 6i, −73 + 6(i+1)) mm and y ∈ [−107 + 6j, −107 + 6(j+1)) mm, which is voxels `3i..3i+2` × `3j..3j+2` of one axial plane. Its centre is (−70 + 6i, −104 + 6j) mm.

### `slices[10]`: `{k, z, outline, familyOutline, cells, n, roi}`
- **Selection.** The mask spans voxel planes k = 8..72 (z = −48..+80 mm). The build takes 10 single 2 mm axial planes at an exact 6-plane spacing, inset equally from both ends: k = 13, 19, …, 67, i.e. **z = −38, −26, −14, −2, 10, 22, 34, 46, 58, 70 mm (12 mm apart)**. All 18 ROIs appear in at least one slice.
- `outline`: polygons of the mask in that plane, as `[[outerRing, hole1, …], …]`. Each ring is a flat `[x0,y0,x1,y1,…]` in mm, implicitly closed. Method: marching squares (`skimage.measure.find_contours`, level 0.5, voxel-centre lattice, zero-padded), even-odd assembly of rings into polygons with holes (shapely), Douglas-Peucker simplification at **1.0 mm** (`shapely.simplify(1.0, preserve_topology=True)`), and removal of fragments under 1 mm². Vertices lie on the half-voxel lattice, so coordinates are integers in mm. 2–10 polygons and 23–95 vertices per slice.
- `familyOutline`: `{semantic, auditory, control}`, built the same way on the plane restricted to each family's parcels. This draws crisp family borders on a plate.
- `cells`: flat `[i0,j0,i1,j1,…]` of every 6 mm cell containing at least one in-mask voxel of that plane. There are 784 cells in total (48–115 per slice). The cells cover every in-mask voxel of every sampled plane (checked).
- `n[c]`: number of in-mask voxels in cell c (1..9), which serves as coverage. Use it for opacity or to drop thin edge cells.
- `roi[c]`: `rois` id of the parcel holding the most voxels in the cell (ties go to the lower id). The cell's family is `rois[roi[c]].family`. 22 of 784 cells straddle two parcels. All others are a single parcel.

### `maps[6]`: `{model, lang, layer, layerDepth, shared, specific, delta, roi}`
- Order: xlmr/en, xlmr/fr, xlmr/zh, nllb_encoder/en, nllb_encoder/fr, nllb_encoder/zh. `layer` and `layerDepth` come from each `manifest.json` (`selected_layer`, `layer_depth`).
- `shared[s][c]`, `specific[s][c]`, `delta[s][c]`: int8 values aligned with `slices[s].cells`. **Value = int8 × `grid.scale[key]`** (or `cellValue(map, key, s, c)`). Units are mean held-out Fisher-z, and `delta` = SHARED − SPECIFIC.
  - Sources: `shared_mean_z.nii.gz`, `specific_mean_z.nii.gz`, `shared_minus_specific_mean_z.nii.gz`.
  - Resampling: the mean of the in-mask voxels of the 3×3 block in that one plane. Voxels outside the mask are excluded.
  - Quantization: round-to-nearest with scale 0.0015 z (shared/specific share one scale, as in the paper's Fig. 8) and 0.001 z (delta). The maximum error against the NIfTI cell mean is 0.00075 / 0.0005, checked over all 14 112 values.
  - Note that the repo's NIfTIs are themselves stored as uint8 with per-file scaling by nibabel, with a storage step of 0.00016–0.00066 z depending on the file. So a voxel value can sit up to about 0.00033 z from the exact group mean. Worst case, a `maps` value sits about 0.0011 z from the exact parcel mean.
  - Ranges across all six maps (z): shared 0.0135..0.1680, specific −0.0075..0.1080, delta 0.0020..0.1110. At the representative layers no parcel has a negative delta.
- `roi`: `{shared[18], specific[18], delta[18]}`, the **exact** group means (6 dp) per ROI at that map's layer. Source: `group_level_roi_results.parquet`, rows `model, language, layer_index = layer, condition ∈ {shared, specific}`, column `mean_z`. `delta = shared − specific`. Use these in readouts. They are the values the projected maps encode before storage quantization.

### `layerCurves[6]`: `{model, lang, layers, depth, family: {semantic|auditory|control: {shared[], specific[]}}}`
- Source: `group_level_roi_results.parquet`, `condition ∈ {shared, specific}`, column `mean_z`. `layers` = `layer_index` (XLM-R 0..12, NLLB 4, 6, 8) and `depth` = `layer_depth` (= layer/12).
- Transform: the unweighted mean over the family's ROIs (8 semantic, 6 auditory, 4 control) of the group mean_z, at 6 dp. Every cell has the same subjects (n = 31/28/35), so this equals the group mean of the subject-level family means, which is how the confirmatory statistic is aggregated (`encoding/xlmr_roi_pipeline.py::_confirmatory_effects`). The family Δz curve is `shared − specific`.
- Check: the mean of the semantic Δz curve over 0.33 ≤ depth ≤ 0.83 (XLM-R layers 4–9, NLLB layers 4/6/8) reproduces every H1 `effect` to 6 dp.
- No family-level SE per layer is stored in the repo, so none is included.

### `confirmatory`: `{h1[6], h2[6], auditory[6], holmFamilySize: 12, midLayerDepth: [0.33, 0.83]}`
- `h1`: the six confirmatory SHARED − SPECIFIC tests (semantic family, middle-late layers). Source: `confirmatory_effects.parquet`, rows `hypothesis = H1_shared_gt_specific_semantic`. Fields map as `effect` = `mean_delta_mid`, `ciLow`/`ciHigh` = `ci_low`/`ci_high` (95% bootstrap, 10 000 resamples), `se`, `dz`, `pPerm` = `p_perm` (one-sided sign-flip, 10 000 permutations), `pHolm` = `p_holm_primary` (Holm across all 12 primary tests) and `n` = `n_subjects`. Same values as `REPO/outputs/tables/table03_main_confirmatory_stats.csv`.
- `h2`: the same fields for `H2_semantic_minus_auditory` (semantic Δz − auditory Δz).
- `auditory`: the auditory-family Δz_mid. Source: `roi_family_effect_panels.parquet`, `panel = auditory` (effect, CI, p_perm, n). It has no Holm column because it is not a primary test.

### `roiStats[6]`: `{model, lang, n, shared[18], specific[18], mismatched[18], delta[18], qFdr[18]}`
Per-ROI middle-late averages (the paper's Table 4 / Sec. 4.4 source), aligned with `rois`. Source: `roi_condition_stats.parquet` columns `mean_z_shared`, `mean_z_specific`, `mean_z_mismatched`, `delta_shared_specific` (6 dp) and `q_fdr` (3 significant figures; BH-FDR, `viz/tables.py`). These are averages over middle-late layers, **not** the representative-layer values in `maps[].roi`.

### `headline`
- `subjects`, `subjectsTotal`, `runsPerSubject`, `trSec`, `durationMin`: from `REPO/outputs/tables/table01_dataset_summary.csv`.
- `h1Positive`, `h1HolmSignificant`, `h2HolmSignificant`: counts over `confirmatory`.
- `representativeLayer`: from `robustness_representative_layers.parquet`.
- `representativeBase`: from the `robustness_cell_results.parquet` rows `canonical_representative_layer_base`, giving the semantic Δz at the representative layer per cell. This equals the mean of `maps[].roi.delta` over the 8 semantic ROIs (checked: 0.068072, 0.067228, 0.039286, 0.055122, 0.051825, 0.033046).
- `robustness[6]`: from `robustness_summary.parquet` and `robustness_cell_results.parquet` (positive cells out of 6, plus the negative cells with their effect).
- `geometryCoupling[6]`: from `geometry_brain_coupling.parquet` (`rho_spearman`, `p_perm`, `n_layers`).

### `cellValue(map, key, sliceIndex, cellIndex)`
Returns `map[key][sliceIndex][cellIndex] * grid.scale[key]`.

## Parcel identity (`rois[].nVoxels`, `slices[].roi`, `familyOutline`)

The repo's resampled atlas (`data/interim/roi/harvard_oxford_cortical_resampled_to_lppc_bold.nii.gz`) and `roi_metadata.parquet` are **not present** offline, and nilearn's Harvard-Oxford cache is not on this machine. The parcel of every voxel was instead **recovered exactly from the six projected maps**. Each map fills parcel `roi_index` with that ROI's group (shared, specific, shared − specific). Each in-mask voxel's 18 values (6 maps × 3 volumes) were matched to the 18 ROIs' exact group means from `group_level_roi_results.parquet`.

The match is unambiguous:
- The best match is always within 0.4995 storage steps on every one of the 18 dimensions.
- The nearest competing ROI is at least 42.7 steps away.
- Labelling each map alone gives the identical assignment in all six.
- Every L parcel lies at x < 0 and every R parcel at x > 0.
- The two precentral parcels (4296 + 4149 = 8445 voxels) match the single quantized shared value they share in the XLM-R/EN map, which holds 8445 voxels.

## Headline numbers for the readout

- **Main result (H1):** the semantic-family middle-late SHARED − SPECIFIC Δz is positive in **6/6** model × language tests, and **all 6 survive Holm correction** (Holm p = 0.0012 each; one-sided sign-flip p_perm = 0.0001; Holm over 12 primary tests).
  - XLM-R: EN **0.0609** [0.0483, 0.0738], FR **0.0589** [0.0484, 0.0697], ZH **0.0324** [0.0255, 0.0393]
  - NLLB-200 encoder: EN **0.0415** [0.0323, 0.0505], FR **0.0429** [0.0340, 0.0517], ZH **0.0194** [0.0132, 0.0255]
  - Effect sizes dz: 1.02 to 1.97
- **H2 (semantic > auditory shared advantage):** 0/6 survive Holm. Auditory/STG regions also carry a strong shared advantage, for example XLM-R FR R_pSTG Δz 0.1096. This is the paper's mixed result. State it neutrally.
- **Per ROI:** 48/48 semantic ROI rows positive and FDR-significant. SHARED > MISMATCHED_SHARED in 48/48 semantic and 36/36 auditory rows.
- **Layer profile:** XLM-R semantic Δz peaks at layer 7 (EN 0.0681, FR 0.0672) and at layer 10 for ZH (0.0395). NLLB peaks at layer 8 in all three languages (0.0551, 0.0518, 0.0330).
- **Sample:** LPPC (OpenNeuro ds003643), 94 listeners (31 EN, 28 FR, 35 ZH), 9 runs each, TR 2.0 s, about 94–99 min of story. 1323 aligned EN–FR–ZH sentence triplets (paper Sec. 3.2; this number is not in `data.mjs`).
- **Models:** XLM-R-base (13 layers, hidden size 768) and the NLLB-200-distilled-600M encoder (hidden size 1024; layers 4/6/8 analysed). 18 Harvard-Oxford ROIs in 3 families.
- **Decomposition:** SHARED u = leave-target-out mean of the other two languages' normalized embeddings. SPECIFIC v⊥ = residual orthogonalized to u (paper Eqs. 1–3).
- **Robustness:** the representative-layer effect keeps its sign in 6/6 cells under 4-lag FIR, no acoustic nuisance, no pitch nuisance and 2-sentence context, and in 5/6 under last-token pooling.

## Not available

- **A whole-brain or cortex silhouette.** The only mask in the repo is the union of the 18 ROI parcels. If the figure needs a brain shell, no repo data supports it, and the ROI-union outline is the honest outline.
- **The resampled atlas NIfTI and `roi_metadata.parquet`** (`REPO/data/interim/roi/`) are missing, and the Harvard-Oxford atlas is not cached offline. Parcel identity was recovered exactly from the projected maps instead (see above), so the per-cell family is real.
- **Voxelwise encoding maps** do not exist. The paper is ROI-first, and the voxelwise-within-ROI robustness target is summarized only per cell.
- **Projected maps at other layers.** There are only 6 maps, at XLM-R L7 and NLLB L8.
- **Family-level SE/CI per layer.** Per-ROI `se_z` exists in `group_level_roi_results.parquet` but was not added. The confirmatory CIs cover the middle-late band only.
